# An `%ei` answer in the browser, with the SciLog posts it cites

`%ei -b` shows the last answer as a page. Click a citation and you get the
SciLog post as it looks in SciLog, with its images and tables, between the
posts written just before and after it.

```text
%ei -b what trigger settings did we use for the tapedrive?
%ei -b
```
