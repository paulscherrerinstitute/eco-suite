# Steer cam_west from your phone

In the web app, a network PTZ camera (`cam_west` and co.) has a live
view: switch on **Control**, then tap to center, drag a box to zoom, or use
zoom ±, wide and home. All viewers share one connection to the camera, and a
slow tunnel drops frames instead of lagging behind.
