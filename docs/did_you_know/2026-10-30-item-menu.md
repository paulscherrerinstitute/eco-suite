# Every row of a property grid has a ⋯ menu

Each Adjustable, Detector or sub-assembly row of a property grid (eco
desktop, `.widget()`, Jupyter) ends in **⋯** (in Qt also a right-click on the
name). It offers what that item supports: its other views, a live strip
chart, the archiver history from 10 minutes to 7 days, indicator gadgets, its
memories, details (channels, unit, limits), and in Qt copying the name or PV
names and **Find IOC**. The setpoint entry now sits right next to the
readback.

```python
mono.widget()          # then ⋯ on the theta row -> archiver history -> 1 day
```
